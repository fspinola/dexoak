Design & asset credits

This site uses an original implementation and layout. 4D CodeBench inspired the restrained chaptered teaser, generous spacing and section-progress navigation. CHORD, OmniRetarget and SPIDER informed the research storytelling and interactive comparisons. No external website template or source code was copied.

Research content comes from the supplied DexOAK manuscript and presentation. Real-robot footage is extracted from the original presentation and encoded at up to 1920 pixels wide, preserving its frame rate. The static header samples five poses spanning the complete spoon-transport sequence, with a faint human–robot overlap only in the middle pose and the forearm cropped at the wrist. Simulator views render actual meshes, forward kinematics, reference trajectories and recorded policy states in the browser.

The cutting optimizer seed and fine-tuned trajectories are archived research outputs. The graph animation rebuilds Delaunay connectivity with a reduced set of surface anchors for clarity. The separately labeled single-frame optimization is a fresh SLSQP re-solve of the graph objective with joint and wrist bounds; it is not the original complete seed-generation optimization trace. Visualization interpolates sampled recorded frames.

OakInk Allegro views retain the original renderer’s fixed alignment between simulation and demonstration coordinates. Inspire uncapping uses the verified DexOAK rollout and original figure time mapping. The original renderer’s ManipTrans Inspire baseline is not labeled or shown as DexOAK. ARCTIC views retain the articulated-object kinematics and verified residual-policy state dumps.
