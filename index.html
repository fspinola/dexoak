The method

Learn the interaction.
Reuse the retargeter.

Optimize training references once. Retarget new demonstrations with a forward pass.

01

Optimize seed references

Match hand–object graph Laplacian coordinates by optimizing robot wrist pose and joints.

02

Learn the retargeting function

Train a feed-forward network on the optimized references, conditioned on the human, object, and previous robot state.

03

Refine contact

Fine-tune with surface-contact, object-part, contact-direction, collision, and temporal losses.

04

Refine dynamics

Residual RL corrects the kinematic reference to execute the interaction under physics.
